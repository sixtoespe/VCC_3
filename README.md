# 🌌🪐 NovaQuest Interactive

### ✨ Explore the Universe. Learn Something Stellar! ✨

Welcome to **NovaQuest Interactive**, a fictional software company specializing in space exploration and astronomy education. Our website concept lets you tour the solar system, discover distant galaxies, and take part in virtual space missions. 🚀🌠

> **Project status:** Demo / In Development 🚧. Features in this README describe the planned experience; they may not yet be implemented.

## 🌟 Overview

**NovaQuest Interactive** makes learning about the universe exciting, engaging, and accessible to aspiring astronauts and space enthusiasts of all ages. Our interactive platform helps you learn about planets and stars, explore celestial objects, and test your knowledge along the way.

From your first look at the night sky to your hundredth simulated mission, **NovaQuest Interactive** aims to help you keep exploring. 🔭❤️

**Industry:** Space & Education  
**Project type:** Interactive astronomy education platform (concept)  
**Target audience:** Aspiring astronauts, students, educators, and space enthusiasts of all ages  
**Mission:** Make learning about the universe exciting, engaging, and accessible to everyone.

## ✨ Features

- 🪐 **Solar System Explorer:** Explore the planets and learn about the stars.
- 🌌 **Galaxy Discovery:** Journey to distant galaxies and learn what makes each one unique.
- 🗺️ **Interactive 3D Star Maps:** Navigate the sky in three dimensions.
- 🛰️ **Spacecraft Simulations:** Experience how spacecraft work and fly.
- 🧑‍🚀 **Virtual Space Missions:** Take part in missions designed for aspiring astronauts.
- 📝 **Educational Quizzes:** Test what you have learned.
- 🔭 **Celestial Object Database:** Look up planets, stars, galaxies, and more.
- 📱 **Responsive Design:** Designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## 🎮 Usage

**Example journey: Plan your first mission!** 🚀

1. 🏠 **Visit the home page** to see featured destinations.
2. 🪐 **Explore the solar system** and pick a planet to learn about.
3. 🗺️ **Open a 3D star map** to find stars and galaxies.
